Hi, I'm Shelly, and I make things!

I'm a self-published children's book author, illustrator and handmaker based in the heart of Texas.

  • In 2010 she wrote and illustrated her first children's book, These White Feathers, as a college project at UTSA, where she studied special education.
  • After graduating she taught life skills to students with multiple and severe disabilities.
  • In 2021 her nephew was born, and she wrote Trampoline Dreaming for him. It is set in Leakey, Texas, where her Hill Country childhood was.
  • She visits schools, hosts read-alouds and stuffs envelopes for the Snail Mail Club.
The love of reading and learning is priceless. Aunt Shelly, Voyage San Antonio, October 2024
